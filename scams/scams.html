Common Types of Online Scams

Learn how common scams work, identify their warning signs, and know what you can do to protect yourself.

🎣
PHISHING

Phishing Scams

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords, banking details or other sensitive information.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Suspicious links
  • Urgent requests
  • Unknown sender
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs

Example

"Your bank account will be blocked. Click here immediately to verify your account."

🛡️ Never enter sensitive information on links received through unexpected messages.
💳
PAYMENT

Payment Scams

Scammers trick victims into sending money through fake payment requests, QR codes or fraudulent transactions.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Unexpected payment request
  • Fake QR codes
  • Pressure to pay immediately
  • Unknown payment accounts

Example

"Scan this QR code to receive your refund."

🛡️ Remember: receiving money usually does not require entering your UPI PIN.
💼
JOB SCAM

Fake Job Scams

Fake recruiters promise jobs with high salaries and then ask candidates to pay registration, training or security fees.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Guaranteed high salary
  • Registration fee
  • No proper interview
  • Unprofessional email address

Example

"Congratulations! You are selected for a ₹60,000/month job. Pay ₹2,000 registration fee."

🛡️ Genuine employers generally don't demand money to give you a job.
🔐
OTP SCAM

OTP Scams

Scammers pretend to be bank employees, customer support agents or officials and ask for OTPs or verification codes.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Someone asks for your OTP
  • Fake customer support
  • Urgent verification request
  • Unknown phone numbers

Example

"I am calling from your bank. Please tell me the OTP to cancel the transaction."

🛡️ Never share an OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.
🏆
PRIZE

Lottery & Prize Scams

Fake lottery or reward messages claim that you have won money or a valuable prize and ask for payment or personal information.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • You never entered the lottery
  • Unexpected prize notification
  • Processing fee request
  • Requests for bank details

Example

"Congratulations! You won ₹10 lakh. Pay ₹5,000 processing fee to receive it."

🛡️ Don't pay money to receive an unexpected prize.
🪪
KYC

KYC Scams

Fake messages claim that your bank account, SIM card or digital wallet will be blocked unless you complete an urgent KYC update.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Account blocking threats
  • Urgent KYC update
  • Unknown links
  • Requests for OTP or PIN

Example

"Your KYC has expired. Update immediately or your account will be blocked."

🛡️ Complete KYC only through the official bank or service application.
📱
UPI / QR SCAM

UPI & QR Code Scams

Scammers use fake payment requests, QR codes or UPI messages to trick people into sending money.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Unexpected UPI payment request
  • Unknown QR code
  • Pressure to scan a QR code
  • Request for UPI PIN

Example

"Scan this QR code to receive your refund."

🛡️ Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
🚔
DIGITAL ARREST

Digital Arrest Scams

Scammers pretend to be police officers or government officials and threaten victims with arrest or legal action.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Fake police or government officials
  • Threat of arrest
  • Video call pressure
  • Demand for money

Example

"Your Aadhaar has been linked to illegal activity. Pay money immediately to avoid arrest."

🛡️ Real authorities do not conduct arrests through video calls or demand money to avoid arrest.
📈
INVESTMENT SCAM

Investment Scams

Fake investment schemes promise very high or guaranteed returns and encourage victims to transfer money.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Guaranteed high returns
  • Investment pressure
  • Unknown trading platforms
  • Requests to transfer money

Example

"Invest ₹10,000 today and get guaranteed returns of ₹50,000."

🛡️ Verify investment platforms and companies before investing.
📦
COURIER SCAM

Courier & Parcel Scams

Scammers claim that a parcel linked to you contains illegal items or requires a payment before delivery.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Unknown parcel notification
  • Fake customs charges
  • Threat of police action
  • Urgent payment request

Example

"Your parcel has been stopped by customs. Pay ₹3,000 immediately to release it."

🛡️ Verify the courier company using its official website before making any payment.
💰
FAKE LOAN APP

Fake Loan App Scams

Fake loan applications promise quick loans and may collect personal information or demand unusual fees.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Instant loan promise
  • Unusual processing fees
  • Requests for excessive permissions
  • Unknown loan application

Example

"Get an instant ₹2 lakh loan. Pay ₹2,000 processing fee first."

🛡️ Check the lender and app details before sharing personal or financial information.
👤
SOCIAL MEDIA

Social Media & Impersonation Scams

Scammers create fake profiles or pretend to be another person to obtain money, information or account access.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • New or suspicious profile
  • Unexpected money request
  • Requests for personal information
  • Pressure to move to another platform

Example

"Hi, I'm your friend. I lost my phone. Please send ₹5,000 urgently."

🛡️ Verify the person's identity through another trusted communication method before sending money.
